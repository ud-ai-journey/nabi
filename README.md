## Install

1. **Download** [Nabi.dmg](https://github.com/ud-ai-journey/nabi/releases/latest/download/Nabi.dmg), open it and drag
   the butterfly into **Applications**.
2. **Open it once.** Nabi isn't on the App Store yet, so macOS asks you to confirm: open **System Settings → Privacy &
   Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
3. **Allow Accessibility** when Nabi asks (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility). That's how it hears
   ⌃⌘ and sees what you've selected; it never reads what you type.

Then select any file and press ⌃⌘.

## What it does

- **Formats:** photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF, GIF), PDFs, Word, Pages, Markdown, HTML, video (MP4, MOV, HEVC,
  GIF, WebM) and audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC)
- **PDFs:** to Word & Pages, searchable scans, password lock/unlock, split & pick pages, watermark, smaller
- **Video & audio:** auto subtitles, 9:16 for Reels, trim, 2× speed & slow-mo, rotate, smaller files
- **Photos:** remove backgrounds, text behind, beautify screenshots, crop, resize, watermark, palette, read QR codes
- **Privacy:** blur private info, redact PDFs, strip hidden location and camera data
- **Everywhere:** Finder, Photos, open documents, the clipboard, drag & drop, right-click, watch folders
- **Little luxuries:** type to find any option, and Copy · AirDrop · Show right from the butterfly

Everything runs on your Mac. No uploads, no account, no ads.

*Nabi* (나비) is Korean for butterfly.
